# Code of Conduct

The Wisdoverse Forge project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as the standard of behavior for everyone who participates in this project:
contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and people interacting in any project
forum.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
review threads, and any other channel where the project's community
interacts. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to `conduct@wisdoverse.example`.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and safety of the reporter
of any incident.

For the full set of enforcement guidelines and the four-tier consequence
ladder (Correction, Warning, Temporary Ban, Permanent Ban), see the upstream
Contributor Covenant text at the link above.
